Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use the Study Planner application ("App", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Our App is designed to respect your privacy. The information we collect is minimal and mostly stays on your device:

  • Local Data: Your study schedules, tasks, goals, and progress are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers.
  • Push Notifications: We may request permission to send you push notifications for study reminders. You can turn these off in your device settings at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for providing and improving the App's core functionality, such as reminding you of your study goals and showing your progress.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our App may use third-party services (like Google AdMob for banner ads). These third-party ad networks may collect anonymized data such as your IP address or Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. We do not share your personal study data with them.

4. Data Security

Since your primary data is stored on your device, its security depends on your device's security features. We recommend keeping your device locked with a passcode or biometric authentication.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
